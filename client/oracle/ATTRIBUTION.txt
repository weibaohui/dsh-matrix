Oracle-bone (甲骨文) glyph data in this directory is derived from:

  Li, Q.-S. & Bian, Y.-L. "An Interpretable Parametric Representation and
  Open Dataset for Oracle Bone Script." npj Heritage Science (2026).
  https://github.com/aylqs2025/oracle-bone-jgw-1203
